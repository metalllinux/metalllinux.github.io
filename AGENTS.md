# Agent Guidance

## Developer Commands
- **Build site**: `bundle exec jekyll build`
- **Git sync**: `git pull --rebase origin main` (Required due to Syncthing sync)

## Content Conventions
- **Language**: Strict US English (`-ize`, `-or`, `-er`).
- **Posts (`_posts/`)**: 
  - No `layout`, `author_profile`, or `show_date` in front matter (handled by `_config.yml` defaults).
  - Filename: `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`.
- **Linux Journey (`_linux_journey/`)**:
  - Mostly auto-generated by `scripts/process_linux_journey.py`; avoid hand-editing if source exists.
  - **No H1 headers** or bare date lines in the body (causes duplicate H1s).
- **Navigation**: Always update `_data/navigation.yml` when adding new pages or notes.

## Repository Quirks
- **Syncthing**: Ignore `SFConflict` files; they are untracked noise.
- **Collections**: `_linux_journey` and `_notes` are custom collections with specific permalinks and layouts.
