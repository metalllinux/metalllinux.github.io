Video games have been a passion of mine for as long as I can remember, and over the years that enthusiasm extended into writing about them.

## Game Articles

I wrote a number of game reviews and articles for [Cane and Rinse](https://caneandrinse.com/author/howard-van-der-wal/), a podcast and website dedicated to in-depth coverage of video games.

One of my favourite pieces I wrote for them is **[Gaming On Linux](https://caneandrinse.com/gaming-on-linux/)** — an article exploring what it is actually like to play games on Linux, covering compatibility, tooling, and the state of the platform for everyday gaming.

## Game Development

I worked in quality assurance on two VR titles developed by [Force Field Entertainment B.V.](https://www.mobygames.com/company/10963/vertigo-studios-amsterdam-bv/) in Amsterdam, both published by Oculus VR. My full credits are on my [MobyGames profile](https://www.mobygames.com/person/822940/howard-van-der-wal/).

**[Landfall](https://www.mobygames.com/game/84597/landfall/) (2017)** — A post-apocalyptic tactical twin-stick shooter for PC VR built in Unreal Engine 4. Players command a single soldier across multi-zone missions with a variety of loadouts, weapons, and deployable mechs called Striders. The game supports solo and co-op campaign play as well as 1v1 and 2v2 versus modes. Landfall received an 86% average from critics and won first place at the 2017 Dutch VR Awards in the Games and Apps for VR and AR Headsets category.

**[Term1nal](https://www.mobygames.com/game/89018/term1nal/) (2017)** — A cyberpunk stealth game for Samsung Gear VR built in Unity. Players take on the role of hacker Flynn Lightman, remotely controlling an android to infiltrate a corporation across twelve story missions. Gameplay involves navigating security patrols, issuing commands to a companion robot dog, and completing first-person hacking mini-games at terminals.
